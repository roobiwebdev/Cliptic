# Storytelling & Scriptwriting Playbook

Reference knowledge for identifying and structuring high-performing short-form video
stories. All formats are told from the creator's first-person point of view.

## The Top 10 Storytelling Formats
1. Hero's Journey (1st POV Problem → Solution)
2. Personal Learning / Epiphany (1st POV Insight)
3. About Me (Origin Story)
4. Before and After (Transformation Reveal)
5. Goal / Dream Journey
6. Challenge
7. Win (Victory Announcement)
8. Day In The Life
9. Personal Update
10. Lesson From Others (Mentor Story)

---

## 1. Hero's Journey (1st Person POV Problem → Solution)

**Description:** A first-person transformation arc that solves a problem or overcomes a challenge.

**Starting Point:** A core problem leading to a transformation arc.

**Storytelling Strategy:**
- Hook
- Establish character and problem (build context)
- Explain failed attempts (exaggerate the problem)
- Introduce a novel solution (trying something different)
- Explain the solution (why it worked and what the success was)
- (Optional) CTA

**Secret Sauce:** It's all about getting the viewer to emotionally relate to you as the character experiencing a common pain. If they feel the pain you felt, they'll be invested in the solution you used to overcome it.

**Templates:**
1. **Intro** — establish the hero and the problem.
   - "X days/years ago I [problem you were experiencing]."
   - "I was a [role] struggling with [problem], and I had no idea how to fix it."
2. **Inflection Point** — highlight the pain from the problem.
   - "Because of this I was [pain points/symptoms]."
   - "It got so bad that I [lowest moment: 'couldn't sleep' / 'almost gave up' / 'was completely broke']."
3. **Rising Action** — failed attempts to fix it.
   - "I tried [failed solution #1], [#2], and [#3] — nothing worked."
   - "For [X months/years] I did everything they said: [common advice]. Still no results."
4. **Climax** — introduce the solution.
   - "Finally after [X months/years] of no results I figured out the one thing that actually worked: [solution]."
   - "Then I discovered [solution/resource], and everything changed."
5. **Falling Action** — the success from the solution.
   - "When I started doing this I [results/social proof/success]."
   - "Within [timeframe] I went from [before] to [after]."
6. **Resolution** (Optional) — CTA.
   - "Because of this experience I started [business/service] so I could help people just like me achieve [dream result]."
   - "Now I help [target audience] go from [pain point] to [dream result]. [CTA]."

## 2. Personal Learning / Epiphany (1st Person POV Insight)

**Description:** Frame a personal victory, then teach how it was achieved. Lead with proof/solution before teaching.

**Starting Point:** A core learning the creator wants to share.

**Storytelling Strategy:**
- Hook
- Establish the desired result (with proof — show the end state)
- Explain the process to get there (solution breakdown)
- (Optional) Restate why this solution is different from the common one
- (Optional) CTA

**Secret Sauce:** You need extremely strong visible proof (screenshots) that creates a curiosity hook ("how did they do that?"). Then you must have a non-obvious solution — otherwise people write off your result as an outlier that isn't replicable.

**Templates:**
1. **Hook (Proof)** — lead with the result or bold claim.
   - "I [impressive result] in just [timeframe] — here's exactly how."
   - "This one change got me [result]. Let me show you what I did."
   - "[Screenshot/proof]. Everyone keeps asking me how I did this."
2. **Backstory** — brief context on where you were before.
   - "Before this, I was [previous state] and I thought [old belief]."
   - "For [X months/years] I was stuck at [plateau] because I was doing [wrong approach]."
3. **The Insight** — the core lesson or realization.
   - "Then I realized [realization] — and that's what changed everything."
   - "The biggest lesson was [lesson]. Here's what that actually looks like:"
   - "The mistake I was making was [mistake]. The fix was [fix]."
4. **Breakdown** — how you applied the insight, step by step.
   - "So here's exactly what I did: First [step #1], then [step #2], and finally [step #3]."
   - "I followed this simple process: [framework]. Step 1 is [...]. Step 2 is [...]. Step 3 is [...]."
5. **CTA** — bridge to action.
   - "If you want the full breakdown, [CTA: 'comment LEARN' / 'link in bio' / 'follow for more']."
   - "This is exactly what I teach inside [offer]. [CTA]."

## 3. About Me (Origin Story)

**Description:** The creator's personal backstory explaining the "why" behind their brand or mission.

**Starting Point:** A background or origin story the creator wants to tell.

**Storytelling Strategy:**
- Hook
- Establish context of normal life
- Introduce the epiphany or change moment
- Break down how that change shaped the path forward
- (Optional) CTA

**Secret Sauce:** Usually pinned to the profile. These build personal trust and connection — they lean personal, not professional expertise.

**Templates:**
1. **Intro** — where your story begins.
   - "Hi I'm [name], and [X months/years] ago I was a normal guy/girl, working as a [role], living a normal life."
   - "Before all of this, I was just a [relatable identity] doing [relatable activity]."
2. **Conflict** — what set you on this path.
   - "Well, all up until [life-changing event]."
   - "Then one day [trigger event] happened, and I couldn't ignore it anymore."
3. **Epiphany** — the realization that changed your perspective.
   - "Because of this I realized [epiphany]."
   - "That moment made me understand that [realization]."
4. **Change** — the action you took.
   - "Which led me to [action], and that leads me to today. Now I [dream result], and [positive side effect]."
   - "So I [bold action]. Fast forward to today — I've [current result] and [lifestyle change]."
5. **Purpose** — the deeper "why" driving your brand.
   - "[Dream result] changed my life, and I made it my mission to help [target audience] go from [pain] to [dream result] too."
   - "Now everything I do is about helping [who] achieve [what] without [common pain]."

## 4. Before and After (Transformation Reveal)

**Description:** Built entirely around the contrast between two states, with a dramatic reveal. Visual format that relies on the gap between "before" and "after" for impact.

**Starting Point:** Two contrasting states (before and after) you want to showcase.

**Storytelling Strategy:**
- Hook
- Establish the "before" state (relatable or dramatic)
- Build brief tension/anticipation (transition beat, music build, or countdown)
- Reveal the "after" state (dramatic cut synced to audio or visual transition)
- (Optional) Brief context on time elapsed or effort involved
- (Optional) CTA

**Secret Sauce:**
- The power is in the size of the gap between the two states — a bigger, more surprising gap = more watch time, saves, and shares.
- The transition moment is the emotional peak — sync it to a beat drop or trending sound.
- Keep it short — the best are 15–30 seconds because the format doesn't need explanation.

**Templates:**
1. **Hook** — tease the transformation.
   - "[X months/years] of [effort] in [X seconds]."
   - "This is what [subject] looked like before vs. after [action]."
   - "Watch this transformation."
2. **Before State** — show the starting point.
   - "This is where I started: [before state]."
   - "[X months/years] ago, this was my reality: [before visual or metric]."
   - (Or: no words — show the before state with a text overlay.)
3. **Transition** — the dramatic cut synced to audio (no script; sync to a beat drop, trending sound, or snap/clap).
4. **After State** — reveal the result.
   - "And this is [subject] now."
   - "[X months/years] later."
   - (Or: no words — show the after state and let the gap speak.)
5. **Context** (Optional) — brief note on time or effort.
   - "[X months] of [effort]. Worth every second."
   - "All it took was [action] for [timeframe]."
6. **CTA** (Optional) — drive next action.
   - "Want to see how I did it? [CTA]."
   - "Follow for the full breakdown."

## 5. Goal / Dream Journey

**Description:** Introduce a long-held dream or ambitious goal and share where you are on the path. Unlike a Challenge (defined rules, bounded timeframe), this is an ongoing, open-ended aspiration. The audience follows along.

**Starting Point:** A long-term dream or big goal the creator is pursuing.

**Storytelling Strategy:**
- Hook
- Introduce the dream/goal and its origin (why it matters to you)
- Share the moment you decided to seriously pursue it
- Give a progress update (where you are, what you've done so far)
- Share what's next (upcoming milestones, timeline)
- CTA (invite participation — "follow to see if I can pull this off")

**Secret Sauce:**
- The dream must feel ambitious enough that the audience genuinely wonders if you'll achieve it.
- Share vulnerable moments about whether you'll make it.
- The best versions feel like you're letting the viewer in on something deeply personal, not just announcing a project.

**Templates:**
1. **Introduce Dream** — origin of the dream/big goal.
   - "Ever since I was [age] I've dreamed of [dream]. Each time I [action], I think about how cool it would be to [dream]."
   - "For as long as I can remember, I've wanted to [dream]. I think about it every [day/week/month]."
2. **Pursuit of Dream** — when you started to seriously act.
   - "After years of dreaming, on one random [weekday] I [realization/life event] and decided it was now or never, so I started [action]. I was [vulnerable feeling] but I did it anyway."
   - "Then [X weeks/months] ago, I finally stopped talking about it and [first action]. I was terrified, but I knew if I didn't start now, I never would."
3. **Progress Update** — where you are on the journey.
   - "So far I've [action], and now I'm about to [action] which involves [steps]. To achieve [dream] by [date] I need to finish this in [timeframe]."
   - "Here's where I'm at: I've [progress]. Next I need to [next step]. The deadline is [date]."
4. **CTA** — invite participation.
   - "Want to see if I can get [action] done on time? [CTA: 'follow to watch' / 'comment YES if you think I can']."
   - "I'm going to document the whole thing. Follow along and hold me accountable."

## 6. Challenge

**Description:** A mission or goal the creator has completed or is actively completing (like a vlog meets a DIY, focused on a specific mission).

**Starting Point:** A challenge or mission goal the creator wants to complete.

**Storytelling Strategy:**
- Hook
- Establish context of the challenge
- Build a plot arc toward completing it (with conflicts and obstacles)
- Resolution or cliffhanger

**Secret Sauce:**
- Make the challenge objective extremely grand.
- Add high-intensity complications or roadblocks along the way.

**Templates:**
1. **Hook** — state the challenge and stakes.
   - "I'm going to [challenge] in [timeframe]. Here's what happened."
   - "Is it possible to [ambitious goal] starting from [starting point]? I tried it."
   - "I gave myself [X days] to [challenge]. No shortcuts."
2. **Rules/Setup** — establish the constraints.
   - "The rules: [rule #1], [rule #2], and [rule #3]."
   - "Starting point: [baseline]. Goal: [target]. Deadline: [date]."
3. **The Journey** — build the arc with conflicts and obstacles.
   - "Day [X]: [progress/setback]. Things were [going well/falling apart] because [reason]."
   - "Then [complication] happened and I had to [pivot]. I almost [near-failure moment]."
   - "By day [X] I was only at [progress]. I needed to [milestone] in [remaining time]."
4. **Resolution or Cliffhanger** — did you make it?
   - "Final result: [outcome]. I went from [start] to [end] in [timeframe]."
   - "And with [X hours] left… [cliffhanger]. Follow to see part 2."

## 7. Win (Victory Announcement)

**Description:** Celebrate a specific achievement with proof. Distinct from Personal Learning (which teaches backward from a result) and Personal Update (general news): a Win announces and celebrates an achievement, not how you did it.

**Starting Point:** A specific achievement or milestone you want to announce.

**Storytelling Strategy:**
- Hook
- State the win with proof (screenshot, footage, tangible evidence)
- Brief emotional context (what it means to you, why it matters)
- (Optional) Quick acknowledgment of the journey/sacrifice
- (Optional) CTA (challenge the audience or invite them to the next goal)

**Secret Sauce:**
- Wins work best when the proof is undeniable and visual (a screenshot, a notification, a physical result).
- Keep it short. The best wins feel spontaneous and raw.

**Templates:**
1. **Hook** — state the win immediately.
   - "It finally happened: [win]."
   - "We just [achievement] and I'm still in shock."
   - "This is the moment I [milestone] — let me show you."
2. **Proof** — show the evidence.
   - "Look at this: [proof]."
   - "Here's the screenshot: [proof]. [X months/years] ago this number was [old number]."
   - (Or: no words — just hold up the proof.)
3. **Emotional Beat** — what this means to you.
   - "You have no idea how long I've worked for this. [X months/years] of [sacrifice]."
   - "I almost quit [when/why]. This is why I didn't."
   - "This was the goal I set [X months/years] ago when I was [previous state]. It's real now."
4. **Acknowledgment** (Optional) — brief nod to the journey.
   - "From [before] to [after]. Wild."
   - "None of this happens without [key factor: 'showing up every day' / 'the people around me']."
5. **CTA** (Optional) — channel the energy forward.
   - "Next goal: [milestone]. Follow to watch."
   - "If you're working toward [similar goal], keep going. It's worth it."

## 8. Day In The Life

**Description:** A day-in-the-life scenario from a first-person POV.

**Starting Point:** A day's worth of events.

**Storytelling Strategy:**
- Hook
- Establish context (character, scene, objective)
- Introduce a complication or challenge (tension)
- Break down the solve, or share your thinking on how to solve it
- (Optional) CTA

**Secret Sauce:** You want someone to like your personality or want the life you have — play into that. Show something surprising and unexpected (people don't expect you to do things a certain way).

**Templates:**
1. **Hook** — establish identity and context.
   - "A day in the life of a [age] year old [role/identity]."
   - "What a typical [weekday] looks like when you [lifestyle detail]."
   - "This is what it actually looks like to [aspiration: 'run a business from your laptop' / 'work 4 hours a day']."
2. **Morning** — set the tone.
   - "I start every day with [routine/habit] because [reason]."
   - "First thing: [action]. Most people don't know I [unexpected detail]."
3. **Core Work** — show what you actually do.
   - "Today's biggest priority is [task/objective]. The challenge is [complication]."
   - "Midway through [task], [problem] happened so I had to [pivot]."
4. **Resolution / Wind Down** — close the day.
   - "End of day. Here's what I got done: [summary]. Tomorrow I need to [next priority]."
   - "This is what [lifestyle] actually looks like. It's not perfect, but it's [honest feeling]."

## 9. Personal Update

**Description:** Share a personal update in your life or mission from a first-person POV.

**Starting Point:** An update the creator wants to share.

**Storytelling Strategy:**
- Hook
- Establish context on you and the situation (character, scene, objective)
- Give the update (include moments of tension or conflict)
- Rationalize the decision (explain the why)
- (Optional) CTA

**Secret Sauce:** Let people see behind the curtain into your life. These aren't for authority/trust — they're for community building.

**Templates:**
1. **Hook** — tease the update.
   - "I need to tell you something: [teaser of update]."
   - "Something big just changed in my [life/business/brand] and I want to explain why."
   - "I've been keeping this quiet for [X weeks/months], but it's time."
2. **Context** — set up the situation.
   - "For the past [X months/years] I've been [previous state/plan]."
   - "You might remember that I was [previous situation]. Well…"
3. **The Update** — deliver the news.
   - "As of [date/recently], I'm [the change]. Here's why."
   - "I've decided to [decision]. It wasn't easy because [tension/conflict]."
4. **Rationale** — explain the why.
   - "The reason is simple: [core reason]."
   - "I realized that [insight], and once I saw it I couldn't un-see it."
   - "At the end of the day, [value/priority] matters more to me than [tradeoff]."

## 10. Lesson From Others (Mentor Story)

**Description:** The creator tells someone else's story — a mentor, client, peer, or public figure whose experience is the lesson. The creator is the narrator/student, not the hero.

**Starting Point:** Someone else's story or experience that taught you something valuable.

**Storytelling Strategy:**
- Hook
- Introduce the person and why they matter (credibility/context)
- Describe their situation or struggle (make it vivid)
- Reveal the lesson or insight they taught you
- Show how you applied it and what changed (bridge to your own results)
- (Optional) CTA

**Secret Sauce:**
- You position yourself as a student and curator, not a self-promoter.
- Make the other person feel like a real character the viewer can picture, not a vague reference.
- Name them if possible, describe the specific situation, and let the lesson land as a direct quote or vivid moment rather than a generic takeaway.

**Templates:**
1. **Hook** — introduce the person and the lesson.
   - "I learned something powerful from [person/role]."
   - "Here's the best advice [figure] ever gave me."
   - "[Person] told me one thing that completely changed how I [area]."
2. **Situation** — describe their struggle or context.
   - "They were struggling with [problem]."
   - "At the time, they were [situation: 'about to close the business' / 'starting over from nothing' / 'at the top of their game']."
   - "When I met them, they had just [event/context]."
3. **The Lesson** — reveal the insight.
   - "They told me: '[golden lesson/quote].'"
   - "Their advice was simple: [lesson]. And it changed everything."
   - "The one thing they said that I'll never forget: '[quote].'"
4. **Application** — how you applied it and what changed.
   - "When I applied it, I [result]."
   - "I took that advice and [action]. Within [timeframe], I [outcome]."
   - "Following that advice, I went from [before] to [after]."
5. **CTA** — share the lesson with the audience.
   - "This one lesson can change everything for you too. [CTA]."
   - "If this resonated, save it. You'll need it on a hard day."
   - "Who taught you the most? Comment below."

## How to use this playbook (for clip selection)

When evaluating a segment of a longer video for short-form potential, identify which
of these 10 story formats the moment most resembles. A segment is more clip-worthy
when it maps cleanly onto one of these arcs — it has a recognizable hook, a middle
that builds, and an ending that resolves or pays off. Use the format's structure to
judge where a clip should truly begin (the hook beat) and end (the resolution beat),
and to write titles/hooks that match how that format wins attention.
